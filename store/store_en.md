## Full description

📚 **Build your English vocabulary, one day at a time!**

Worday helps you learn English words from A1 to C2 with daily vocabulary sessions, spaced repetition, quizzes and a personal wordbook. Create a learning habit and keep track of your progress.

🧠 **Smart spaced repetition with SM-2**
Worday uses the SM-2 spaced repetition algorithm to schedule vocabulary reviews. Words you find difficult can return for another review, helping you reinforce what you learn.

🎯 **Daily goals and quizzes**
Choose a daily word goal that fits your routine. Test your knowledge with short multiple-choice quizzes and review your results.

🤖 **AI-powered example sentences**
When you are online, request AI-generated English example sentences for selected words. Explore how vocabulary is used in different contexts.

📖 **Your personal wordbook**
Save useful words, search your collection and filter words by learning status.

📊 **Track your learning progress**
Review your study activity, answer accuracy and learning streak.

📴 **Learn offline**
Your vocabulary catalog, core learning sessions, quizzes and saved words are available on your device. Online features such as AI-generated examples require an internet connection.

✨ **Make a little progress every day with Worday.**  
Start building your English vocabulary today!

*AI-generated content may not always be accurate. Choose a learning level and daily goal that match your needs.*
